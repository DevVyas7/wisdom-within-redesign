# Wisdom Within Redesign

Build a complete responsive website for “The Wisdom Bharat – Awaken The Wisdom Within” using https://thewisdombharat.com/ as the source of truth.

IMPORTANT: Redesign and improve the website, but preserve the real logo, founder identity/photo, book cover, contact details, existing reviews/testimonials and verified information. Never invent credentials, testimonials, statistics, prices or claims.

SERVICES: Keep ONLY the services from the original website:
• NLP
• Reiki
• Garbhsanskar
• Life & High-Performance Coaching
Do NOT add unrelated services and do NOT create a Workshops page.

HEADER:
Logo | Home | About | Services ▾ | Programs | The Book | Testimonials | FAQ | Contact | Book a Consultation
Services dropdown: NLP, Reiki, Garbhsanskar, Life & High-Performance Coaching.

DESIGN:
Premium, warm Indian wellness aesthetic. Cream, sacred red, charcoal, muted gold and sage. Elegant serif headings, clean sans-serif body text, subtle mandala/Indian details, excellent whitespace and modern typography. Avoid an overly spiritual or generic stock-photo look.

HOMEPAGE:
Keep it concise and visually balanced. Use:
Hero → short introduction → 4 service cards → founder → Garbhsanskar highlight → programs → book → selected testimonials → simple “How it works” → FAQ → final consultation CTA.

Give EACH of the 4 services equal visual importance and a relevant high-quality image. Do NOT make Garbhsanskar dominate the homepage.

IMAGES:
Use the real founder photo and real book cover from the original website. Use appropriate authentic imagery for NLP, Reiki, Garbhsanskar and Life Coaching. Avoid repetitive/generic meditation images.

TESTIMONIALS:
Create a polished dedicated Testimonials page and a smaller section on Home. Reuse only genuine testimonials/review photos available from the original website. Use attractive photo + quote + name cards. Do not fabricate reviews.

PAGES:
Home, About, Services, NLP, Reiki, Garbhsanskar, Life & High-Performance Coaching, Programs, The Book, Testimonials, FAQ, Contact, Book a Consultation, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions.

FUNCTIONAL:
Make all navigation, dropdowns, buttons, forms, WhatsApp/contact links and booking CTA functional. Mobile-first, responsive, accessible and SEO-friendly.

PRIORITY:
Clean premium layout, less homepage content, equal service representation, authentic imagery, strong typography and easy navigation. Build the complete website in one coherent design.
